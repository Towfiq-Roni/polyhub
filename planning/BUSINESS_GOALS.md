## Goals (priority order)

1. Financial traceability — every money movement is ledger-backed and auditable; company/account balance is always computed, never manually maintained.
2. Operational visibility — one consistent view of clients → projects → milestones → deliverables → payments.
3. Reduced tool sprawl — retire ad-hoc spreadsheets and disconnected tracking.

## Success criteria

- All 17 MVP capabilities in BRD §6 functioning end-to-end (users/RBAC, teams, CRM, projects/milestones/deliverables, manual payments, receivables/payables, company/project financials, audit trail).
- Zero ledger discrepancies: every account/company balance always reconciles to its posted journal entries, and the trial balance always balances to zero, with no manual override needed, after 1 month of real use.
- Payment status never observed to block milestone/work progression.
- Every sensitive/financial write has a corresponding audit log entry.

## Constraints / invariants

- $0 recurring infrastructure cost target; no external hosting budget.
- Up to 5 concurrent users, monthly usage-burst pattern — no high-throughput scaling need.
- All monetary calculations exact — no rounding drift.
- Full double-entry general ledger (chart of accounts + balanced journal entries) is the financial system of record — supersedes the earlier simple IN/OUT ledger design (ADR-0011/DEC-064 reversed).
- Posted journal entries immutable — corrections only via linked reversing entry, never edit/delete.
- No free-form manual journal entry — every entry is system-generated from a defined business event.
- Single tenant, single company, single currency (৳/BDT).
- All payments manual — no payment gateway/bank integration in v1.
- BDS compensation supports both fixed amount and percentage-of-contract-value.
- Out of scope v1: hard payment-gate config, payroll/tax engine, HR/attendance/recruitment/procurement/inventory, marketing automation, task management/Gantt/time-tracking, multi-company/multi-currency/multi-tenancy, AI features.
